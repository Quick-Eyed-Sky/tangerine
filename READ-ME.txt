TANGERINE 1.3.0 — sequences in slow light

Unzip the download and keep the complete folder together.
Python 3 is required. There are no packages or models to install.

On a Mac, double-click "Tangerine II.command".
On macOS/Linux, run ./run.sh, or python3 scripts/serve.py --open.
Keep the Terminal window open while listening or recording.
Click Start the vine in the browser. Start at 60 BPM or go as slow as 6.

If macOS asks you to confirm a downloaded launcher, read docs/first-opening.md.

Record to this computer saves WAV progressively into Recordings/, next to the
launcher, until Stop & finish files. Long recordings continue into consecutive
one-hour WAV files. Keep the computer awake and check free disk space.

Recover a recent passage downloads the last seconds or minutes: up to the
20 minutes still in memory. Download the files before closing the page.
The online demo's browser recording is limited to 30-minute passages.
